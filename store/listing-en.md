## Full Description (max 4,000 characters)
**Timed Chess. No Check. Pure Vibe.**

**Vibe Chess** is a fast-paced, minimalist timed chess variant designed for quick, high-energy tactical matches. Every piece moves just like standard chess—including castling, en passant, and pawn promotion—with one bold twist: **there is no check, no checkmate, and no stalemate.**

### How Vibe Chess Works
- **No Check or Checkmate:** You can move your King into an attacked square or leave it under attack. Even the King can be captured like any other piece, and the game keeps going!
- **Every Piece Counts:**
  - Pawn = **1 point**
  - Knight = **2 points**
  - Bishop = **3 points**
  - Rook = **4 points**
  - Queen = **5 points**
  - King = **7 points**
- **Beat the Clock (1 or 2 Minutes):** Each player starts with either 1 minute or 2 minutes on the chess clock (no increment). Only the side to move has a running clock.
- **Win on Points When Time Runs Out:** The instant either player's clock reaches `0:00.0`, the game ends immediately. The player with more points remaining on the board wins! Equal points result in a draw.
- **Live Winning Probability Bar:** Instead of distracting point tallies during live play, Vibe Chess displays a smooth real-time **Winning Probability Bar** (White % vs. Black %) alongside captured-piece trays. Final board points are revealed on the Result screen.

### Game Modes
1. **Single Player vs. Computer:**
   - Choose from 3 tailored AI difficulty levels: **Easy**, **Medium**, or **Hard**.
   - Play as **White**, **Black**, or **Random**.
   - Time-aware AI adapts to the final 10 seconds of the clock.
2. **Multiplayer (Pass-and-Play Offline):**
   - Play face-to-face with a friend on a single phone or tablet.
   - Includes an optional **"Rotate opponent's pieces 180°"** setting (enabled by default) for comfortable tabletop play.
